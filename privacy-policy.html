Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Bali Moto Adventures ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Who We Are

Bali Moto Adventures operates premium motorcycle expeditions across Indonesia. For any privacy-related queries, contact us at ride@balimotoadventures.com.

2. Data We Collect

We may collect the following personal data when you contact us or interact with our website:

  • Name and phone number (when you message us via WhatsApp)
  • Email address (when you submit a contact form or newsletter signup)
  • Country of residence and travel dates (provided voluntarily during booking enquiries)
  • Name, email, phone number and trip preferences (when you submit one of our Instagram or Facebook lead forms)
  • Device and browsing data (collected automatically via cookies and analytics tools, only after you accept them — see Section 8)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to your enquiries and process tour bookings
  • To manage our CRM and follow up on leads
  • To send relevant information about our tours (with your consent)
  • To improve our website and marketing performance
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Analytics and Tracking

We use the following third-party tools to understand how visitors use our website:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — measures how visitors use the site. It only loads after you accept cookies in our cookie banner (see Section 8); if you reject, it does not run.
  • Meta Pixel — tracks conversions from our Facebook and Instagram ads. Governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

5. WhatsApp Communications

When you contact us via WhatsApp — including after submitting one of our Instagram or Facebook lead forms, which hands your enquiry straight to WhatsApp — your messages may be processed by an automated assistant powered by Claude (Anthropic). Your name, email, phone number and conversation data are stored in our CRM to manage your enquiry. We do not share this data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who help us operate (e.g. payment processors, hosting)
  • Authorities if required by law

7. Data Retention

We keep enquiry and lead data for as long as needed to respond to you and manage a possible booking, and for up to 24 months after your last contact with us if no booking is made, after which it is deleted. You may request earlier deletion at any time (see Section 9).

8. Cookies

Essential cookies needed to run the site are always on. The site also keeps a few notes in your browser's own storage that identify no one: your cookie choice, and whether a sign-up pop-up has already been shown to you or an itinerary unlocked, so that we do not ask you again. Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4) and advertising cookies (Meta Pixel) only load after you accept them in the cookie banner shown on your first visit — you can reject them there instead. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page: it reopens the banner, so withdrawing your consent is as easy as giving it.

If you reject or withdraw your consent, Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel are not loaded and we delete the Google Analytics and Meta Pixel cookies stored under our domain (_ga, _ga_*, _gid, _fbp, _fbc). We cannot delete cookies that Google or Meta keep under their own domains; you can remove those in your browser settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of what was processed before you withdrew it.

Video on our home page: the video preview is an image served from our own website, and nothing is sent to YouTube while you browse. If you press play, the YouTube player is loaded from Google: Google then receives your IP address and information about your device and browser, and may store cookies or similar data on your device, under Google's Privacy Policy. We embed the player in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. If you prefer not to connect to YouTube, simply do not play the video.

California residents: under the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to opt out of the "sharing" of your data for cross-context advertising, which is what the Meta Pixel and the audiences built from it are used for. To opt out, reject cookies in the banner or at any time through the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page, or email ride@balimotoadventures.com with "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" in the subject line.

9. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for cookies, use the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at ride@balimotoadventures.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at balimotoadventures.com/privacy-policy.html.
