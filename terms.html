These Terms and Conditions govern your participation in tours operated by Bali Moto Adventures. By making a booking, you agree to these terms in full.

1. Bookings and Deposits

A deposit of $500 per person is required to confirm your reservation. Payment is then made in two instalments:

  • 50% of the total tour price, two months (60 days) before departure. The $500 deposit counts towards this instalment.
  • The remaining balance, two weeks (14 days) before departure — or in cash on arrival, if you prefer.

Bookings made when one of those dates has already passed require that instalment at the time of booking.

2. Cancellation Policy

  • More than 30 days before departure: free cancellation — everything you have paid is refunded in full, deposit included

We strongly recommend purchasing comprehensive travel insurance that covers trip cancellation.

Bali Moto Adventures reserves the right to modify itineraries due to weather, road conditions, local regulations, or safety reasons. In such cases we will provide a comparable alternative at no additional cost. No refunds are issued for itinerary modifications beyond our control.

Participants must hold a valid motorcycle licence appropriate for the bikes provided and be in good physical health. You are responsible for informing us of any medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate safely.

5. Insurance

Travel and motorcycle insurance is not included in the tour price. Two options are available:

  • Full-Risk Insurance: $275 per person — covers all motorcycle damage during the tour

We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance covering medical evacuation, trip cancellation, and personal liability.
