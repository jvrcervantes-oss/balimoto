Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Bali Moto Adventures is committed to ensuring that our website, balimotoadventures.com, is accessible to all visitors, including people with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, which is the standard referenced by the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) and the harmonised European standard EN 301 549.

Conformance Status

This website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. "Partially conformant" means that some parts of the content do not yet fully conform to the accessibility standard. We are actively working to close the remaining gaps.

Measures We Have Taken

  • The complete booking flow — from browsing tours to starting a secure payment — can be operated with a keyboard alone.
  • Interactive controls show a visible focus indicator when navigating by keyboard.
  • Form fields carry programmatic labels, and form errors are announced to assistive technology.
  • Dialogs and overlays can be dismissed with the Escape key.
  • Images carry text alternatives, and animations respect the "reduced motion" preference where supported.

Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some content may not yet be fully accessible:

  • Some custom dialogs do not yet constrain keyboard focus while open.
  • Some decorative animations and video content may lack full alternatives.
  • Colour contrast on a small number of decorative elements may fall below the recommended ratio.

We are addressing these issues as part of our ongoing accessibility programme.

Assessment Method

The accessibility of this website was evaluated by self-assessment, carried out on 13 July 2026, covering the main purchase flow (home page, tour pages, booking calculator and payment entry point) against WCAG 2.1 Level AA criteria.

Feedback and Contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this website. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, or need information from this site in an alternative format, please contact us:

  • Email: ride@balimotoadventures.com

We try to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days.

Enforcement

If you are located in the European Union and are not satisfied with our response to an accessibility issue, you may contact the market surveillance or enforcement authority responsible for the European Accessibility Act in your member state.
