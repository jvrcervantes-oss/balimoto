Bali — West Coast
03
Seminyak → Tibubiu
60 km · 3h

Into the water before the jet lag wears off, then your blessing for the trip. You finish the day on black volcanic sand.

Today's Highlights

●Lazy River tubing at Penarungan

04
Tibubiu → Depeha
110 km · 4h30

The full width of Bali, south to north, over the highlands. The longest day of the trip and the most beautiful.

Today's Highlights

●Pura Siwa, a temple well off the tourist trail
●Fresh fish at Warung Pesisi on the Bali Sea

Questions

Frequently
Asked

What's included in the price?
Selected hotels, breakfast, every activity on the itinerary, inter-island transfers, Balinese massages, temple ceremonies, surfing lesson, river rafting, snorkelling and cooking class. Not included: a road guide, petrol, international flights, visa fees and personal travel insurance.
Do I need to ride a motorcycle?
No. The tour offers four transport options: shared motorcycle (Bike × 2), solo motorcycle (Bike × 1), self-drive car, or private car with driver. You choose what fits your comfort level.
What level of fitness is required?
Medium. The itinerary includes river rafting, surfing lessons, ridge walks and snorkelling sessions. The driving days are long but comfortable, especially in the car options. You don't need to be an athlete — just curious and ready for adventure.
What are the hotels like?
Selected hotels carefully chosen for character and location — beachfront in Tibubiu, Bali Sea views in Depeha, glamping on lava fields in Batur and stays in Ubud and the Nusas.
Do I need a visa for Indonesia?
Most European passport holders can enter Indonesia visa-free for up to 30 days. For longer stays a Visa on Arrival is available. Always check the latest requirements for your nationality before booking.
How does the deposit and payment work?
A $500 per person deposit secures your spot. The remaining balance is due 60 days before departure. You can pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or SEPA bank transfer (Wise).
Can I join as a solo traveller?
Absolutely. Choose Bike × 1 or a car option to travel at your own pace. Solo travellers are very welcome — you'll meet the rest of the group along the way.
How is 4 Islands different from 7 Islands?
Same trip, shorter. 7 Islands adds two days in the Gili Islands off Lombok and runs 13 days; 4 Islands takes those out and runs 10, going straight from Bali to the three Nusa islands. If you are tight on holiday or want the lower price, this is the one.
